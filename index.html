MASE divides absolute error by the client's training-only weekly seasonal error. Values are comparable across client sizes; raw kW scores remain in the full report.

Variation across days

1,000 moving-block resamples of seven observed days, within each test fold. Dates are resampled jointly across clients. This interval describes this fixed cohort, rather than unseen utility sites.

Clock and missing-data decisions

UCI retains 96 quarter-hour slots on every day. Whole DST transition days are excluded from targets; forecasts crossing those dates are also excluded. Local clock labels are preserved.

Initial zero runs are excluded using the first training period. Subsequent zeros remain valid readings. Missing inputs use up to six hours of forward fill, then the training median.

Input-loss mode is a controlled stress test: 5% random loss plus a 24-hour outage in each test month. The original observed targets and models stay fixed.

Interpretation

A compact linear model fits this deliberately simple simulation well. That does not establish superiority on real grids.

Prediction bands use held-out residuals. Temporal dependence and distribution shift can reduce coverage. All three feeders share a generator, so they are not independent external validations.

What this proves

Verification detects edits, reordering and truncation relative to the saved checkpoint. A local attacker who rewrites both the chain and checkpoint defeats local verification.

The separate Solidity registry can anchor the checkpoint and manifest digest in an append-only contract controlled by authorized writers.

Blockchain execution evidence

The included contract was compiled and exercised on an ephemeral local EVM. This browser does not connect to a blockchain.

Tests cover authorized anchoring, rejected overwrites, access control and writer revocation. No raw meter records or personal data are placed on-chain.

Method & evidence

A small, complete research system with inspectable assumptions.

01

Generate

180 days of hourly readings for three fictional feeders: demand, EV load, PV output and noisy weather forecasts. Injected anomalies occur only during testing.

02

Predict

Calendar harmonics, exogenous weather forecasts and strictly past load features feed a standardized Ridge model. Gradient boosting and a seasonal baseline are evaluated separately.

03

Monitor

Calibrated residual bands and alert thresholds expose deviations. Aggregate capacity checks show scenario headroom without claiming voltage or network-security analysis.

04

Attest

Prediction records carry integer watts, timestamps and model/data digests. A local hash chain supports inspection; a Solidity contract supports external anchoring.

System design

GridLedger connects time-series forecasting, scenario exploration and verifiable prediction records in one reproducible workspace.

Version 2 adds a licensed UCI electricity benchmark on 20 clients, four time windows and three horizons. The synthetic studio retains EV/PV scenarios, labelled injected anomalies and its independent temporal audit. Real-data prediction records and a separate manifest are available in the Real data workspace.
